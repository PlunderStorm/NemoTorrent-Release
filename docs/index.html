Private by design, not by setting

NemoTorrent does not have a "use VPN" switch you can forget to turn on. The VPN is the only way out.

Always behind your VPN

Every download is bound to the WireGuard tunnel address. No tunnel, no traffic: it never falls back to your normal connection.

Two-level kill-switch

The app stops downloads the moment the tunnel drops, and the Windows firewall blocks download traffic outside the tunnel, even if the app crashes.

No DNS or IP leaks

DNS lookups travel inside the tunnel too, never to your internet provider, and IPv6 stays off for downloads. A built-in privacy check shows the exit IP the world actually sees.

WireGuard and NordVPN

Import any WireGuard .conf file, or add NordVPN (NordLynx) servers with your access token, including P2P servers.

Speed profiles

Turbo, Medium, Slow and Night, one click or Alt+1 to 4 away. Schedule them by hour, or slow down on their own while you game or watch a film.

Torrents, magnets and links

Torrent files, magnet links and HTTP, HTTPS and FTP downloads in one list. Optional Real-Debrid support, still inside the tunnel.

No telemetry

No analytics, no tracking, no NemoTorrent account. The app only connects where your downloads, your VPN and the features you turn on need it to.

Signed updates, through the tunnel

Updates are checked only while the VPN is on, and installed only if the signature matches the key built into the app.

Made to be read at a glance

Dark, light and high-contrast themes, adjustable text size, every status with an icon and a word. English and Italian.

Kill-switch

When the VPN drops, everything stops

You do not have to watch it. NemoTorrent watches the tunnel for you, and Windows itself is told to refuse anything that tries to go around it.

  • Downloads pause instantly. The status turns red and says why, in plain words.
  • Firewall rules hold even if the app crashes, so a frozen window can never leak your address.
  • It reconnects on its own and resumes where it left off, if you want it to.

Simple where it matters

Pick a server from the top bar and start downloading. Everything else is there when you need it.

Install in a minute

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a WireGuard or NordVPN account.

Download

Get NemoTorrent_x64-setup.exe from the latest release. An .msi is there too for managed installs.

Install

Run the installer. On first launch NemoTorrent checks for WireGuard and offers to install the official package if it is missing.

Connect

Add a NordVPN server or import your WireGuard .conf, connect, then paste a magnet or open a .torrent.

Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time, because the installer is not yet signed with a code-signing certificate. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway", but only for an installer you downloaded from this site or its GitHub releases page.
